## Research focus

This project investigates how to extract claims from brain tumor AI papers and compare them across publications. I trained and evaluated context-enhanced biomedical claim detectors using paper-level splits and manually verified test data. **PubMedBERT Context achieved 0.8391 macro F1 and 0.9301 AUC.**

For structured claim extraction, deterministic repair and grounding increased macro field-presence F1 from **0.1668 to 0.4850**.

The paper was accepted at AIST 2026, CCIS track. See the [publication details](/publications/). Related data: [BrainTumorClaims](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20364566).
